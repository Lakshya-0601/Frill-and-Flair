About the boutique

Meet thedesigner

One woman, one sewing machine, and a lifelong love for fabric. This is how Frill & Flair was born.

F&F

Hello, I'm the heart of Frill & Flair

I have always believed that clothes should feel like they were made for you, because they should be.

My love for stitching began years ago at home. I watched the women in my family turn plain lengths of fabric into something to celebrate in. I started by sewing for my own family, then for friends, then for friends of friends. Somewhere along the way it stopped being a hobby and became Frill & Flair.

Today I design and stitch sarees, suits, lehengas and blouses for every kind of occasion, from everyday wear to the big wedding day. Every order begins with a conversation about you: what you love, how you move, and where you'll be wearing it.

The frill is the detail: the lace, the tassels, the hand-finished border. The flair is how you feel when you wear it. I'd love to make something for you.

— With love, Mom
