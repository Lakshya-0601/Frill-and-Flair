How it's made

From fabric to flair

No two bodies and no two occasions are the same, so nothing here comes off a rack.

  1. 01

    Choose the fabric

    Pick from the boutique's fabrics or bring your own. We'll talk colour, drape and occasion.

  2. 02

    Measure & design

    Careful measurements and a design sketched around you: neckline, sleeves, borders, finishing.

  3. 03

    Cut, stitch, embroider

    Every seam is stitched in-house. The embroidery, lace and tassels are added by hand.

  4. 04

    Try, tweak, take home

    A fitting to get it perfect, then it's yours, ready for its first celebration.
